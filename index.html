01

How I Form Plots

You could say a plot is born of two ideas: a place and a character. Simply knowing where and who can easily lead to some creative storylines.

Example — Two Ideas, One Plot

Where
At the zoo
Who
A monkey

First thoughts from combining the two

  • The monkey escapes
  • A man discovers a talking monkey
  • The monkey starts a riot
  • The monkey wants a banana
02

How Goals Are Made

Making goals is almost intuitive to looking at the first thoughts.

Example — Turning a Thought into a Goal

Take the idea that “The monkey wants a banana.”

Expounded idea: The monkey notices in the newspaper that the world’s largest banana is on display.

Realized goal: The monkey wants to figure out a way to escape the zoo and reach the world’s largest banana.

Obvious reward: He gets to eat that banana.

03

How Worlds Are Built

Worlds are built not by focusing on worldbuilding, but by focusing on the jobs and needs of the characters and plot. If I were to build worlds intentionally, it would never get done because worlds are far too vast to create quickly.

By focusing on the actors’ jobs in the plot, the world simply falls into place via What They Need | Where They Need It | How They Use It

04

How Dialogues Are Handled

Dialogues are often goal based—either they convey a bridge between not having something and having something, or they are simply there to entertain the listener.

Dialogues don’t just make actions happen; they convey a story about each character involved. Make a bland character and you get bland dialog. Make an extraordinary character and you get an extraordinary conversation.

Scene Example

Underwater, below a floating rubber tire

Character: Max, a fish with a wild childlike imagination

(Max dives into the water below the tire.)

(Max stares at the tire for a moment and his imagination starts to take over.)

(Suddenly Max starts to see a face morph into the tire.)

MAX A monster!

(Max takes his trusty spear and thrusts it at the vicious monster.)

MAX Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaah! STAB!

(WhEEEEeeeeEEEEEeze!)

(The tire deflates and sinks to the ocean floor.)

(Max faces down towards the deflated tire and says:)

MAX Ha! Take vat, monster!

(Max swims down to the tire and uses his spear to cut off two pieces of rubber.)

MAX Monster meat! I wonder how it tastes?

(Max excitedly throws it in his mouth and chews a moment.)

(His eagerness to try something new turns into a look of disappointment.)

(Max reaches back into his mouth and pulls out the rubber slices…)

MAX Maybe someone else will like vis monster meat.

Dialogue Examples

Ace, a marine colonel fish, and Max, a childlike fish.

ACE Private! Stand at attention when you see an officer!

MAX But i’m not~

ACE Don’t question me private! Atten~TION!

(Max quickly stands to attention and puts his fin to his forehead as a held salute)

MAX Y-Yes sir!

ACE What’s your name private?

MAX It’s Max sir.

ACE Very good. At ease private. (Max relaxes)

ACE My name is Ace. And that’s Mr. Ace to you. How long have you been in the marines private?

MAX How long? I didn’t even know vey allowed fish in vu marines.

ACE Not marines! Marines!

MAX I’m not really a marine sir.

ACE You live in the ocean don’t you?

MAX I do now.

ACE Then you’re a marine.

MAX Huh. I didn’t know vat.

ACE PRIVATE!

(Max is surprised)

ACE Did I give you permission to be at ease?

MAX No sir!

(Max stands straight.)

ACE Alright then, at ease.

MAX Phankyou sir.

MAX (excitedly) Mr. Ace sir, could you please tell me about vat spear!?

ACE Most certainly, private. That is my most prized possession. It tells a great story of one of my acts of bravery! It happened like this:

(Story bubble)

I was on guard duty that day and it was my job to protect the riches of Fort Rocks.

In a flash, I was SURROUNDED by TWO HUNDRED fierce fish-eating sharks! It seemed I was done for! But I held onto my trusty spear and charged at the rampaging sharks with full force!

(Story bubble ends)

The battle was won that day.

MAX Wooooow! Ace! You’re my hero!

ACE This spear is my prized possession and I will never be separated from this mighty spear.

MAX Can I have it?

ACE Hmmm. Private, I can see you will do great things one day! I know you will use this spear for greatness!

MAX Hmm??? (Max dreams about using the spear as a skewer with worms on it)

(Ace hands the spear to Max.)

(Max drops to the ground because of the weight of the spear.)

MAX Uggh. Phankyou.
